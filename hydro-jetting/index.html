Main line backing up? Stop water use and call

If sinks, tubs and a basement floor drain all back up, stop every fixture. Do not pour chemicals down the drain. A backed-up main may need a camera look before jetting, since a collapsed pipe can be damaged by high pressure. We take these calls overnight and on weekends, with same-day appointments when available.

While you wait, keep the family out of the basement if sewage is rising and avoid touching standing water.

What hydro jetting actually does

A jetter pushes a hose with a nozzle through the line, spraying water at high pressure in several directions. The spray scours grease, scale and soft roots from the pipe wall instead of poking one hole through the middle. That is why jetting suits repeat clogs.

A cable machine, by contrast, cuts through the blockage but can leave residue behind. Our drain cleaning service covers cable work, and we recommend jetting when the problem comes back within months.

Jetting or cabling

The choice depends on the cause.

SituationBetter first choiceWhy
Hair and paper in one sink.Cable.Fast and enough for a localized clog.
Kitchen line with years of grease.Jetting.Washes grease off the walls.
Roots in a main line.Cable then jetting.Cut roots first, then clean the pipe.
Recurring backup every few months.Jetting after a camera look.Finds and cleans the cause.
Cracked or collapsed pipe.Neither until repaired.Pressure can worsen damage.

A camera inspection tells us which one fits.

Natick sewers and why roots show up

Natick's sewage is treated by the MWRA, and the sewer charge follows your water meter, but the lateral from your house to the street is yours to maintain. Older clay and cast iron laterals have joints that tree roots find. Large trees near Natick Center and along the older streets of South Natick are a regular cause.

Jetting clears root hairs, but a pipe with open joints will fill again. If that is what we see, we show you the footage and discuss sewer line repair before you spend more on cleaning.

What decides jetting cost

Not every line needs it.

Pipe length and diameter. Longer lines take more time.
Access. Cleanouts speed the job. Missing ones add work.
Severity. Heavy grease or root masses take more passes.
Camera work. Inspection before and after is part of a proper job.
Jetting is priced after the camera

Insurance seldom covers cleaning. Once the camera shows the pipe, we name the cost for jetting and any follow-up pass.

Code, permits and licensing for this work

Hydro jetting is a cleaning method, so it falls outside most permit rules, but any pipe repair that follows is subject to 248 CMR. Equipment manufacturers publish pressure and nozzle guidelines for different pipe materials, and we follow them to avoid damaging older lines.
