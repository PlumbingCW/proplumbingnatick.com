Wellesley has two water sources, and that matters

Wellesley gets about 65 percent of its water from the MWRA and about 35 percent from its own wells, ten of them across the Waban Brook and Rosemary Brook aquifers. Three town treatment plants handle corrosion control and iron and manganese removal. Hardness in the town's report ranges from 111 to 493 parts per million, or about 6.5 to 28.8 grains per gallon, with pH from 7.4 to 7.6.

That range is wide because your water depends on which source feeds your street. The practical result is that some Wellesley homes have noticeably hard water that scales water heaters and fixtures, while others have soft water. The town's public works office, at (781) 235-7600 extension 3350, can tell you which supply serves your address.

Source: Town of Wellesley water quality report and MWRA information.
