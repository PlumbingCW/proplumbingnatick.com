Pipe burst? Shut it off first, then call

Go to the main shutoff, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it clockwise. Open the faucet on the lowest floor to let the pipe drain. Cut power to any wet area if you can do it safely from a dry spot. Then call us.

If the main valve will not close, Natick's water division asks residents to call them. After hours, the town points callers to police dispatch. We take burst pipe calls overnight and on weekends, with same-day appointments when available.

Why pipes burst in a Natick winter

Massachusetts cold snaps drive pipes to freeze in unheated spots. Common culprits in Natick are exterior walls, crawl spaces, garages and basements with leaky rim joists. Ice expands inside the line and the split often forms where the ice blocks the flow, so the leak may not show until the pipe thaws.

Older houses with pipes tucked into outside walls are especially exposed. If one frozen pipe split, others in the same wall may be cracked too. We test the whole run before we call the job done.

Repair or replace the section

The fix depends on the damage.

Spot repair

  • Works when the split is short and pipe is otherwise sound.
  • Faster and less invasive.
  • Used for a single frozen section.
  • Not enough if the pipe is corroded.

Replacing a run.

  • Better for corroded or repeatedly failing lines.
  • Avoids repeat failures in the same wall.
  • Takes longer and may involve openings.
  • See our repiping service for whole-house work.

Frozen pipe: what to do and what to avoid

A thaw can create a burst, so go slowly.

SituationSafe moveAvoid
No water at a faucet in a cold snap.Open the tap, warm the area with a hair dryer.Open flame or a torch.
Pipe visibly bulging or split.Shut the main and call.Thawing it yourself.
Frost on an exterior wall pipe.Open cabinet doors, add safe heat.Leaving the house with the heat off.

When in doubt, shut off the water and call.

Preventing the next one

Seal gaps in foundation walls, insulate exposed pipes and leave a slow drip on lines in exposed walls during extreme cold. If you travel in winter, keep the heat on and have someone check the house. We can also add shutoffs that isolate sections so the next failure is smaller.

Damage from a burst often reaches the ceiling below, so after we stop the water, call your insurer. Most policies cover sudden burst damage, but the specifics vary. A plumber's report of what failed helps your claim.

Code, permits and licensing for this work

Repairs to supply piping are plumbing work under 248 CMR and fall to licensed plumbers. After a burst, standard practice is to pressure test the repaired line and document what failed, which also helps with an insurance claim. Ask the Natick building department about permits for any larger rework.

Sources: 248 CMR and the Natick building department for permit questions.
