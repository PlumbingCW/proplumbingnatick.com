Wayland water and sewer: what we know and what to ask

Wayland's Water Division runs a treatment facility at 101 Old Sudbury Road, and you can reach the division at 508-358-3699, or 508-358-4721 after hours. We do not have a published hardness figure for the town, so ask the division what your water is like before you buy a softener or a tankless heater.

Sewer service is also different here. The Wayland Wastewater Management District serves properties along Route 20 from Route 27 to the Town Center area, plus Alta Oxbow, with discharge to the Sudbury River after treatment. That does not mean the rest of town uses a particular system. Ask the Board of Health which system applies to your address before any repair that touches the septic or sewer.

Sources: Town of Wayland water division and wastewater district information.

The river and the March 2010 flood

During the March 2010 floods, a duck boat carried residents to and from their homes in Wayland, according to National Weather Service accounts. Eastern Massachusetts received 7 to 10 inches of rain that month. The event is why local plumbers talk so much about sump pumps and backups.

A battery backup and a clear discharge line are the practical response. If your lot sits low, test the pump before spring and after heavy storms.
