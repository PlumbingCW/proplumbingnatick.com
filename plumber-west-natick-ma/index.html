Plumbing help in West Natick

West Natick is the western end of town, next to Framingham. The West Natick commuter rail station sits off West Central Street, which is Route 135, and the area is described in local references as the most densely settled part of Natick, with condominiums and apartments near the station.

What we know about the neighborhood

Natick's retail hub for the west side includes the Natick Mall and Sherwood Plaza, and local descriptions place post-World War II house developments here, including areas known as Westfield, Pelham and Sherwood. The 1980 Historical Commission survey also records Speen Street as an early link between West Natick and Saxonville.

Natick's own housing figures are town-wide, not neighborhood by neighborhood. The 2025 community profile says about 45 percent of the town's 15,889 homes were built in 1959 or earlier, which tells us that postwar houses are common across Natick. It does not tell us the age or pipes of any single West Natick street.

Because West Natick borders Framingham, some addresses carry a Framingham feel. If you are close to the line, check which town's water and sewer office serves you. Natick's Water and Sewer Division is at (508) 647-6557.

Sources: Wikipedia overview of Natick villages, the 1980 Massachusetts Historical Commission Natick town report and the Natick 2025 community profile.

Shared plumbing, shared responsibility

In a building with shared drains, a clog in your sink can be a symptom of a blockage in the stack, and the stack is usually the building's to maintain. Slow drains in several units on the same line point to the building.

If several neighbors report the same backup, tell the manager before paying for a private cleaning. A single call to the right party avoids paying twice for the same blockage.
