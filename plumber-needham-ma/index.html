Needham water and what it does to your plumbing

According to its own water page, Needham lists hardness at 71.5 milligrams per liter as calcium carbonate, about 4.2 grains per gallon, and a pH of 8.9. That is on the soft side. Tanks scale slowly, and tankless units need less aggressive descaling than in harder towns, though sediment still builds over years.

MWRA lists Needham as partially supplied by its water system. The town's Department of Public Works, at 781-455-7550, can tell you what feeds your street. Needham also asks residents to keep sump pumps out of the sewer, and its inflow and infiltration page calls such discharges illegal. If your sump drains into a floor drain, ask the town what is allowed and move it outside.

Sources: Town of Needham water characteristics and inflow and infiltration pages.
