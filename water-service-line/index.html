Emergency Water Service Line Repair in Natick, MA

The service line brings water from the street main to your house. When it leaks, you may see a wet lawn, low pressure or a meter that never stops. Call and we will help.

Suspect a service line leak? Check these signs first

Look for a soggy, sinking or unusually green patch in the yard between the street and the house, even on dry days. Check your pressure, which often drops when the line leaks. Do the meter test, turning off everything and watching the leak dial. If it spins, water is escaping somewhere.

If the leak is strong, close the valve nearest the meter, and call the Natick Water and Sewer Division at (508) 647-6557 if it will not close. Then call us. We take service line calls overnight and on weekends, with same-day appointments when available.

Who owns what between the street and your house

The town maintains the water main under the street. The line from the main to your house is generally the property owner's responsibility, though the exact point where the town's responsibility ends varies. Confirm with the Natick Water and Sewer Division before you dig or hire out the work.

We can explain what we find, but responsibility questions belong with the town. If the leak turns out to be on the town's side, we tell you right away so you do not pay for something that is not yours.

What Natick's water record tells us

Natick's 2025 water quality report shows lead at the 90th percentile as not detected, with none of 30 sampled homes over the action level, and copper at 0.36 parts per million. That is good news for your tap. It does not tell you what your own service line is made of, since the town's testing samples a set of homes rather than yours.

If your house is old, ask us to identify the line material when we expose it. If it is galvanized or an older type, replacing the whole line rather than patching it often makes more sense. A related issue is high pressure on aging pipes, which can show up through hidden leaks indoors.

Code, permits and licensing for this work

A water service line replacement is subject to the standards in 248 CMR and to the town's water division rules about connection to the main. Confirm the permit path with the Natick building department and the water division before digging, and ask for the line material to be recorded.

Sources: 248 CMR, the Natick Water and Sewer Division and the Natick building department.
