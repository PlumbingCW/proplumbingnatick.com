Ashland water: wells, treatment and what it means

Ashland gets all of its drinking water from five groundwater wells, with an MWRA interconnect at Southborough available in a drought. The town treats the water with ozone, filtration, chloramines and zinc orthophosphate, and its report lists a pH of 7.42. According to the report, lead was 3.6 parts per billion at the 90th percentile and copper 0.309 parts per million, with none of 33 homes over the action level. Hardness was not reported.

For plumbing, the missing hardness figure means you should ask the Department of Public Works, at (508) 532-7954, before you pick a tankless unit or a softener. Zinc orthophosphate is added to protect pipes from corrosion, which is helpful for older plumbing, though it does not replace repiping when lines are failing.

Source: Town of Ashland water quality report.
