Water spraying under the sink? Shut it off first

Look under the sink for two small oval valves, one for hot and one for cold. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If the valves are stuck or will not close, shut the main water valve instead. Then put a bucket under the leak and call.

We take faucet and sink emergencies overnight and on weekends, with same-day appointments when available. Seized shutoff valves are common in old houses, and we replace them during the visit so the next leak is easy to stop.

Common faucet and fixture problems

Most leaks trace to a handful of parts.

Dripping spout.

A worn cartridge, washer or seat inside the faucet.

Leak at the handle.

A failed O-ring or a loose packing nut around the stem.

Low flow.

Mineral buildup in the aerator or a partly closed shutoff.

Water under the sink.

A loose compression nut, cracked supply line or leaking trap.

Shower diverter stuck.

Scale or a failed diverter valve.

Tub spout dripping.

A worn diverter or valve behind the wall.

What to do while a faucet leaks

A few safe steps limit the damage.

  • Close the shutoff valves under the sink, turning them clockwise.
  • Put a bucket or towel under the leak and empty it often.
  • Keep water away from nearby outlets and power strips.
  • Do not force a stuck valve, since a cracked valve makes it worse.
  • Take a photo of the faucet label so we can bring parts.

Repair the faucet or replace it

A fixture's age and finish often decide.

Repair

  • Best when parts are available and the faucet is sound.
  • Often a short job.
  • Keeps the fixture you chose.
  • Wise for newer, quality fixtures.

Replace.

  • Better when the body is corroded or parts are obsolete.
  • A modern faucet can improve flow and reduce drips.
  • Lets you update style.
  • May be cheaper than chasing old parts.

Hard water and fixtures in Natick

Natick's 2025 water report shows hardness around 108 parts per million. That is moderate, but it still leaves scale on aerators, showerheads and the cartridge seats inside faucets. White crust around a spout is a sign. Soaking the aerator in vinegar often restores flow.

If a faucet leaks again soon after a repair, the seat inside may be pitted by scale, and a replacement is the honest answer. For leaks that come from the walls instead of the fixture, see our leak detection page.

Code, permits and licensing for this work

Faucet repair is routine plumbing work under 248 CMR, and parts should meet the maker's published standards. Replacement parts should match the manufacturer's listed cartridge or kit, and shutoff valves replaced during the visit should be rated for potable water. If you plan to move a fixture, ask about permits first.

Sources: 248 CMR and faucet manufacturers' parts guidelines.
