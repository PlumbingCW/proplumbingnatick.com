What this site collects

This website is informational and has no forms. We do not ask you to type your name, address or payment details into a page here. If you email us at contact@proplumbingnatick.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it to answer you.

Phone calls

Our business runs on the phone. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may note your name, address and phone number so we can schedule and complete your visit, and we use that information only for that purpose and for our own records.

Cookies and third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps through Google Maps. Those services may set their own cookies or log your visit under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices
